Before you buy

Warranty Information.

Use the warranty shown on the exact phone listing you are buying.

Warranty differs by listing

Denz does not assume one warranty term for every phone. Where a product listing contains a warranty term, that is the warranty information to confirm for that phone. If the listing says “Confirm with Denz” or does not show a warranty, ask Denz before payment.

Pre-Owned and Cheaper Options

Battery health, cosmetic condition, repaired parts, network restrictions and other known unit-specific conditions should be reviewed before ordering. A disclosed condition forms part of the description of that specific phone.

Faults and consumer rights

A store warranty does not remove rights that apply under South African consumer law. If a phone develops a fault, contact Denz with your order or proof of purchase and a clear description of the problem.

Get written confirmation

If any warranty wording is unclear, get written confirmation from Denz before paying so the warranty attached to your order is clear.
