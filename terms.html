2. Products, prices and stock

3. Ordering and payment

The current checkout prepares your order details and sends them to Denz through WhatsApp. Denz confirms stock, the final amount and the available payment method before payment. The website does not currently ask customers to enter card numbers or banking passwords.

4. Collection and delivery

Collection is available by arrangement. Delivery is charged at a flat R100 through the current checkout. The expected dispatch or delivery timing is confirmed with the customer before payment, based on destination and stock availability. If Denz cannot supply an ordered item, the customer will be informed and any payment already made must be handled in accordance with applicable South African consumer law.

5. Lay-by

Selected phones may be available on lay-by for up to four months. The exact phone, total price, payment schedule and completion arrangement should be confirmed with Denz before the lay-by starts.

6. Warranty and consumer rights

Brand New stock is currently advertised with a 6 month Denz warranty. Any store warranty is additional to rights that customers may have under South African law and does not remove statutory rights relating to defective or unsafe goods.

7. Returns, refunds and privacy

Our return rules are set out on the Returns & Refunds page. How customer information is handled is explained on the Privacy / POPIA page.

8. Contact before payment

If any product detail, delivery term, condition note, warranty term or price is unclear, contact Denz before paying. Written confirmation on WhatsApp or email forms part of the order record.
