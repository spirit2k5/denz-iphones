Delivery & collection

Delivery Policy.

What customers should know before choosing delivery or collection.

Delivery option

Denz lists nationwide delivery as available. The current website checkout adds a flat R100 delivery fee when Delivery is selected.

Address details

For delivery, provide a complete street address, suburb, city, province and postal code together with a reachable phone or WhatsApp number. Check your details before payment.

Collection

Collection can be selected instead of delivery. Collection arrangements should be confirmed with Denz before travelling.

Timing and stock

No fixed delivery time is promised on this page. Denz confirms the expected delivery or collection arrangement based on the destination and current stock. Paid account orders can show status updates such as preparing, ready for collection, out for delivery and completed.
