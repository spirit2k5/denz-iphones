Understand the lay-by before paying.

Denz iPhones lists a lay-by period of up to four months for selected iPhones. Choose the phone first and confirm its price with Denz. The phone is released only after the lay-by has been fully paid. The calculator above can split a phone price into four equal estimates to help you plan.

If the lay-by is not completed after four months, the listed terms state that R500 is kept and the rest of the money is refunded. Confirm the final arrangement directly with Denz before making a payment.

Planning a lay-by clearly

Start with the exact phone you want and confirm its current price before calculating payments. A simple four-part estimate can help with budgeting, but the payment arrangement should still be confirmed directly with Denz so both sides are working from the same phone, price and completion date.

Keep proof of each payment and the agreed phone details until the lay-by is completed. The website explains the published four-month limit and refund rule, while the final arrangement remains something to confirm with Denz before the first payment. If the phone or price changes before an agreement is made, recalculate from the updated amount.
