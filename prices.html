Why prices may differ between iPhones

Two iPhones with the same model name can still have different prices because the storage size, category, condition and listed device details may be different. That is why the website keeps Sealed Box, Brand New, Pre-Owned and Cheaper Options separate instead of presenting every phone as the same type of product.

Use the price list to narrow down your choice, then open the relevant category to see the product information available for that phone. Before paying, confirm the current stock and final amount with Denz. If a model is marked “Ask Denz”, contact Denz for the price.
