Payment Policy.

How online payment and order confirmation work at Denz iPhones.

Secure Paystack checkout

When you choose Paystack, Denz verifies the current product, storage, stock and listed price on the server before creating a checkout. You are then sent to Paystack’s hosted payment page. Denz does not store your full card number or card security code.

When an order becomes paid

Returning to the Denz website after checkout does not by itself mark an order as paid. Payment is recorded as confirmed only after Denz receives and verifies the secure payment notification from Paystack.

Failed or cancelled payments

A failed or cancelled checkout is not treated as a paid order. Do not pay twice if you are unsure of the result; check your order status or contact Denz with your order reference.

WhatsApp ordering

WhatsApp ordering remains available as another option. Confirm the exact phone, storage, colour, stock and final amount with Denz before using any payment method arranged through WhatsApp.

Currency and delivery

Website prices and online checkout amounts are in South African rand (ZAR). Delivery through the current checkout adds the listed flat R100 delivery fee; collection does not add that delivery fee.
